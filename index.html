Modern Frisian breaking
Breaking is a historical sound change in Frisian when a two-vowel sequence develops into a new combination involving [j] or [w] and a vowel. For example, the word skoalle used to be pronounced more like sk[oa]lə, but in many Frisian dialects is now pronounced as sk[wɑ]lə.

It duorre skoften ear't elk klear wie mei it iten.

It hat lang duorre foardat it multyfunksjonele soarchgebou der kaam.

De gearkomste hie fan de middeis oant de jûns let duorre.
